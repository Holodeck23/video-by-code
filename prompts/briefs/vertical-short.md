# Vertical short brief

Make a focused 1080x1920/30fps explanation, initially 15-30 seconds. Confirm the intended platform, viewer and one takeaway. Use the selected style as a full visual system rather than adding decorative borders around landscape content.

Open on a visible problem, surprising result or direct question in the first 1-2 seconds. Move through one mechanism or before/after comparison. End on a takeaway the viewer can read in roughly 1-2 seconds. Do not claim a universal retention formula or invent performance evidence.

Write a compact shot/cue table before coding. Each beat changes the relevant object or reveals evidence. Keep one clear visual centre per beat; a fast pace does not require a cut every second.

Compose vertically from the start. Reserve a central reading area and configurable top/bottom UI exclusions, initially about 180px top and 280px bottom, then verify against the target application's current overlays. Keep captions away from right-side controls. These starting margins are assumptions, not guaranteed platform specifications.

Use approximately 80-120px headlines and at least 48px critical labels at this frame size. Keep the main object large enough to recognise in a phone preview. Reflow diagrams into stacked stages; never simply crop a landscape camera and hope labels survive.

If voiced, lock the actual recording and align cues to it. If silent, make every step understandable from the image and concise text. Optional effects reinforce visible events and do not replace captions or factual context.

Expose canvas id c, a positive window.DURATION, window.__ready and pure window.__renderFrame(t). Use fixed seeds and absolute time. Build the vertical geometry independently of CSS preview scaling; the canvas dimensions determine the encoded output.

Proof the hook, the busiest frame, each transition and the last frame before full export. Test out-of-order seeking. Render with the kit renderer and verify exact dimensions, frame count, colour tags, full decode and audio presence when expected.

Watch the encoded MP4 at actual phone size. Check caption reading time, content under simulated UI overlays, empty pauses, ambiguous arrows and an abrupt ending. Re-edit rather than merely increasing animation speed.

Deliver the vertical page, timing plan, MP4, contact sheet and checks. State the platform overlay assumptions and any untested device behaviour.
