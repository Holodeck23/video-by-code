# Data story brief

Explain one decision or relationship using supplied evidence. Confirm the data source, units, coverage dates, relevant population and what comparison is legitimate. If the dataset is missing or unsupported, ask for it; do not create plausible-looking facts.

Begin with a question, establish a baseline, reveal the measured difference, explain uncertainty or context and finish with the supported conclusion. Fit the length to the reasoning, initially about 25-45 seconds.

Write a claim table linking each statement to a source field, calculation and displayed visual. Validate totals, missing values, denominators and unit conversions before animation. Label synthetic demonstration data clearly on screen.

Choose the chart for the comparison: bars for magnitude, a line for an ordered time series, direct-labelled paths for a process. Use a shared scale across comparisons. If truncating an axis is justified, label it clearly; do not animate deceptive baseline changes.

Apply the selected medium to material and hierarchy while preserving geometric accuracy. Screen-print roughness or hand-drawn edges must not change a numerical value. Keep category colours stable and provide labels or shapes so colour alone does not carry meaning.

Plan cues for baseline, comparison and conclusion. Reveal enough at a time to make the argument understandable, holding the final state long enough to inspect. Counters may animate between values, but label the actual endpoint and avoid implying unmeasured intermediate observations.

Use 1920x1080/30fps initially, with headline around 100px, important labels at least 42px and key figures at least 96px. If a chart needs tiny text, reduce its scope or split the explanation. Place source credit at a readable size when it matters to the claim.

Implement canvas id c, window.DURATION, window.__ready and a pure window.__renderFrame(t). Store chart geometry and cue times as data; use the same values for labels, bars and verification. Optional effects mark reveals without implying stronger statistical certainty.

Before full export, compare rendered positions to expected data values and inspect the hero frame. Verify determinism, full decode, frame count, dimensions, colour tags and audio where present. Then watch the encoded story and check that its phrasing matches what the evidence actually supports.

Deliver the source page, source/calc table, data provenance, cue plan, MP4, contact sheet and checks. Report unresolved uncertainty and distinguish an illustrative example from a validated analytical finding.
